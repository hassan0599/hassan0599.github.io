LocalTunes

An offline-first music player for Android

Your library lives on-device in SQLite, search runs locally, and everything fetched from the internet — MusicBrainz metadata, cover art, legal downloads — is cached so the app keeps working with no network. No accounts, no analytics.

  • Instant local search across songs, albums, artists, genres and playlists — SQLite FTS5 prefix matching with BM25 ranking and a recency boost.
  • Background playback with lock-screen controls, shuffle/repeat, a persisted queue and resume position.
  • Discover via a rate-limited MusicBrainz client with stale-while-offline caching; legal downloads only (Internet Archive, Jamendo) with license labels shown before every download.
  • Persistent, resumable download queue that survives the app being killed — concurrency limits, retries with backoff and a Wi-Fi-only mode.
